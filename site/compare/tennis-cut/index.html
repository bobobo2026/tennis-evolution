Tennis Evolution vs Tennis Cut

Short answer: Tennis Cut is the quickest way to trim dead time on an iPhone — it finds the rallies on the phone by itself. Tennis Evolution is for the next step: a reel of your longest rallies with the hit count, venue and clock time on each one, lossless clips, intact HDR, built on your computer or by an AI agent. Both keep your footage off the cloud.

Choose Tennis Cut if

  • you want the dead time gone with no input at all, on the phone you recorded with.

Choose Tennis Evolution if

  • you want each rally labelled with how many shots it lasted, where and when it was played;
  • you want the longest rallies first, not the whole match in order;
  • you want lossless original clips alongside the reel, and a self-check that the reel decodes cleanly with audio in sync;
  • you work on a computer, in scripts, or through an AI agent.

FAQ

Can Tennis Evolution trim rallies automatically like Tennis Cut?

No. Tennis Evolution needs the rally times as input. It then cuts lossless clips, burns hit count, venue and time labels, builds one reel and verifies it.

Do Tennis Cut and Tennis Evolution upload my video?

Neither does: Tennis Cut processes on the iPhone, Tennis Evolution on your computer.
