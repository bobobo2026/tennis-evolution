Make a tennis highlight video from a long iPhone recording

You filmed a whole session on a phone propped against the fence. Here is how to get a 3-minute reel of the best rallies out of it, with the hit count and time printed on each one, without uploading the footage anywhere or flattening the HDR.

1. Get a list of rallies

You need start and end times for each rally. Three ways work:

  • sliceapp (or any hit-detection app): export its highlight list as text, lines like 27:14 - 28:08 Long rally (40 hits).
  • Your own detector or a spreadsheet: a JSON file {"segments":[{"start":"27:14","end":"28:08","hits":40}]}.
  • By hand: scrub the video and type the times. Hit counts are optional.

2. Install the tool (2 minutes)

brew install ffmpeg
pipx install git+https://github.com/bobobo2026/tennis-evolution

Python 3.11+ and ffmpeg are the only requirements. macOS and Linux.

3. Render the reel

tennis-evolution all IMG_5155.MOV --highlights highlights.txt \
  --top 5 --place "Riverside Tennis Club" --timezone America/New_York --outdir out

You get out/reel_labeled.mp4 (with labels), out/reel_plain.mp4, one lossless clip per rally in out/clips/, and manifest.json with every self-check result.

Why the iPhone HDR look survives

iPhones record HEVC 10-bit with HLG (and often a Dolby Vision layer). Most quick editors either re-encode the clips to 8-bit SDR or drop the colour tags, so the reel looks grey. Tennis Evolution cuts each rally with -c copy on keyframes, so the clips are bit-exact, and encodes the reel once with the BT.2020/HLG tags passed through. Portrait orientation is baked into the picture, so platforms that ignore rotation metadata still show it upright.

What the labels show

Top-left: venue, the wall-clock time of that rally (recording start + offset), and where it sits in the source file. Top-right: the hit count, large. The labels are rendered as transparent PNGs, so they work even on ffmpeg builds without drawtext, and Chinese text renders correctly.

FAQ

Do I have to upload my video?

No. Everything runs on your computer; the site has no upload form.

How long does it take?

About 3 to 5 minutes for a 47-minute 4K-class HEVC source and five rallies on an M-series Mac.

Is it free?

Yes for up to five rallies per reel (with a small watermark). Pro, a one-time $19 key, removes the cap and the watermark.
